InSituGame Assay

In Situ Spatial Evolutionary Game Assay for Inferring Tumor Subclonal Payoff Matrices and Guiding Adaptive Therapy from Intact Biopsies

Luca Celebrano · School of Medicine and Surgery, Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II & TIGEM

Transforming Spatial Biology into Evolutionary Game Theory

InSituGame solves a historic bottleneck in mathematical oncology: rather than guessing game parameters or using artificial 2D in vitro petri dishes, it estimates subclonal frequency-dependent payoff matrices directly from intact patient biopsy microarchitectures (10x Genomics Visium).

Microenvironmental Decoupling

Removes extrinsic physical confounders (hypoxia CA9 and endothelial vascular proximity PECAM1) via multivariable spatial regression to isolate true cell-intrinsic game interactions.

Identifiable 9-Parameter Matrix

Solves collinearity on the frequency simplex through an exact absorbed payoff formulation, ensuring numerical stability across thousands of spatial spots.

Space-for-Time Ergodicity

Formalizes Proposition 1, proving that static spatial snapshot transcriptomics rigorously maps the continuous velocity field of replicator differential equations.

Optimal Transport Validation

Validates inferred subclonal trajectories against Monge-Kantorovich Wasserstein-2 mass transport across serial 3D histological tissue sections.

Lineage Specificity

Demonstrates stable Hawk-Dove coexistence in Breast IDC (61.3% sensitive equilibrium) and Stag Hunt bistability in Cutaneous Melanoma (48.1% tipping point).

Adaptive Therapy Design

Simulates adaptive dosing schedules to suppress competitive release of drug-resistant clones, significantly extending simulated progression-free survival.
